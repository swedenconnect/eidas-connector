eIDAS Connector Compose Setup

Links to the services of the compose setup: the eIDAS connector, the eIDAS simulator and the Sweden Connect test services.

Before you start:
  • The links only work while the setup runs (docker compose up -d in compose/) and local.eidas.swedenconnect.se is mapped to 127.0.0.1 in your hosts file.
  • All HTTPS services use the same self-signed certificate, and your browser shows a warning for it the first time you open each service. Open the connector, the simulator, Test my eID and the test client and accept the warning for each of them before you log in, or a login stops halfway with a certificate error.
  • See compose/README.md for details.

Service Providers

  • Test my eID Start here

    https://local.eidas.swedenconnect.se:12030/testmyeid

    Log in through the connector with a simulated country and see the attributes that the connector sends.

    • SP metadata: https://local.eidas.swedenconnect.se:12030/testmyeid/metadata
    • Signature service metadata: https://local.eidas.swedenconnect.se:12030/testmyeid/metadata/sign

  • Sweden Connect Test Client

    https://local.eidas.swedenconnect.se:12040

    Build SAML authentication requests to the connector in detail and inspect the responses and assertions.

    • Test SP eIDAS metadata: https://local.eidas.swedenconnect.se:12040/saml/metadata/eidas

eIDAS Connector

  • Swedish eIDAS Connector

    https://local.eidas.swedenconnect.se:12010/idp/metadata/idp.xml

    The connector built from this repository. A SAML Identity Provider towards Sweden Connect, and a SAML Service Provider towards the simulated eIDAS nodes.

    • IdP metadata: https://local.eidas.swedenconnect.se:12010/idp/metadata/idp.xml
    • eIDAS SP metadata: https://local.eidas.swedenconnect.se:12010/idp/metadata/sp
    • Health: https://local.eidas.swedenconnect.se:12011/actuator/health
    • Info (countries): https://local.eidas.swedenconnect.se:12011/actuator/info
